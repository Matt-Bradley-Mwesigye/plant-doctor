Care Centre 💚

Simple tips to help you look after your plants.

💧 Watering

Check the soil before watering. Water when the plant needs it, and make sure excess water can drain away.

☀️ Sunlight

Different plants need different amounts of sunlight. Check what your plant prefers before moving it.

🌱 Soil

Use suitable soil for your plant and avoid soil that stays waterlogged for long periods.

✂️ Pruning

Remove dead or damaged leaves with clean tools. Avoid removing too much healthy growth.

🐛 Pest checks

Look under leaves and around stems for insects. Identify the pest before choosing a treatment.

🌿 Plant spacing

Give plants enough room for air to move around their leaves and for their roots to grow.

Remember

A plant's needs depend on its species, age, soil and environment. There is no single care routine that works for every plant.
