✅ What you can do

  • Check the soil before watering.
  • Look at the leaves for yellow or brown spots.
  • Make sure the plant gets enough light.
  • Remove badly damaged leaves if needed.
  • Check the plant again after a few days.

Plant care

Simple things that help your plant stay healthy.

💧

Water

Do not water just because the calendar says so. Check the soil first. If the top feels dry, your plant may need water.

☀️

Sunlight

Plants need different amounts of light. Keep your plant somewhere that matches what it needs.

🌱

Growth

New leaves are a good sign. Check your plant every few days and look for changes.

🪴

Pot & soil

Your pot should let extra water escape. Soil should not stay soaked for a long time.
